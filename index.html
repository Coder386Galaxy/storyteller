🎯 How it works

  1. Hit 📝 Start writing — a passage named start is waiting for you.
  2. Write a scene. For narration/dialogue, use 💬 Continue (readers tap Continue →). For branches, click + Add choice.
  3. Each choice can lead to another passage, to a 💀 death ending, or a 🏆 victory ending.
  4. Click ⚡ Publish, give it a title, description, and any series it belongs to. AI age-rates it (ST-1 through ST-5) and saves it to your library.
  5. Share with a friend using the 🔗 button — they get a code they can paste to play your story.
🔒 Private by default
Everything stays in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded unless you share a code.
🏫 School-friendly flag
Stories without intense content get an ST-1/ST-2 badge so they're safe in class.
👤 Age-aware
Set your birthday once. Mature stories require age verification before opening.
📁 Series
Group sequels & themed stories into series; a story can live in multiple series at once.

✨ Paste Story → AI Finalizes

Paste your writing and your choices below. AI connects everything into a playable CYOA — adding Continue → links where you didn't write choices, filling in missing endings, and cleaning up paths. You can write choices inline with A/B/C labels, or just let AI add them.

How it works:
  • Each paragraph becomes a scene, chained together with Continue → buttons.
  • Lines that look like choices (A/B/C, bullets, numbered) get turned into real branching buttons.
  • AI fills in death and victory endings, connects loose ends, and names scenes.
  • If you don't write any choices, AI adds a few balanced ones automatically.
