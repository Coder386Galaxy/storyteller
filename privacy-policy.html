Storyteller CYOP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Storyteller CYOP ("the app") is a story-building app that runs entirely on your device. We designed it so that we cannot see your data — because your data never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no trackers. Your stories, passages, age setting, votes, comments, library, and settings are stored only in your device's local app storage and are never transmitted to us.

Sharing you control

Stories leave your device only when you choose to share them — for example by copying a share code or link and sending it to someone yourself. Shared story data is packed inside the link/code you send; it is not uploaded to us.

Third-party services

OpenAI (optional): If you optionally enter your own OpenAI API key to use the AI story editor, your story text and your requests are sent directly from your device to OpenAI's API using your key. OpenAI's handling of that data is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy and terms. If you do not enter a key, nothing is sent anywhere.

Stripe (web version only): On the web version, if you choose to subscribe to Storyteller Pro, checkout is handled by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. The iOS app does not offer subscriptions.

Children

The app includes on-device age gates and content ratings. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete any story inside the app, or delete/uninstall the app, and all of your data is permanently erased from your device.
